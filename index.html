Ready to explore underwater worlds as a volunteer for NewSeas? This is the perfect opportunity for anyone to provide support and clean-up efforts that reduce ocean pollution and help protect wildlife of the ocean. Volunteers gain scuba diving and conservation experience while being actively involved in helping make sure our sea life can survive and thrive for many years to come.
